Our story

Built by two people who hated the listings they were shown.

Aditi and Nikhil met while house-hunting for their own families, frustrated by the same thing every buyer knows: photos that hide the damp patch, agents who never answer their own phone, and a fee structure nobody explains upfront.

So they built the studio they wished existed — a small team that walks every address personally, turns down more sellers than it lists, and treats a flat, disclosed fee as a baseline, not a selling point.

Twelve years on, the team has grown to four named specialists, but the rule hasn't changed: if a founder hasn't walked it, it doesn't go on the site.

How we got here

A short, honest history

2014

Two founders, one spreadsheet

Aditi and Nikhil close their first deal for a friend — a plot in Saravanampatti — using nothing but a shared spreadsheet and a lot of site visits.

2017

The studio opens its doors

A small office on Race Course becomes the first physical home for Stackly, and the six-neighbourhood coverage map is drawn up — and hasn't grown since.

2020

Legal & registration brought in-house

Priya joins to lead title review personally, ending the practice of outsourcing legal checks to a rotating cast of external lawyers.

2023

Plot & land specialism

Farhan joins from his family's land brokerage, and Stackly starts handling boundary disputes and plot sales as a dedicated practice.

2026

184 addresses, one flat fee

Today the studio has closed 184 addresses at an average of 11 days to offer — still with a founder walking every listing first.

What we won't compromise on

Three rules, held since 2014

Walked, not scraped

Every address on our books has been personally inspected by a founder — never pulled from a portal feed.

One disclosed fee

No commission games, no hidden buyer charges. The fee is flat, paid by the seller, and stated on the first call.

Honest, even when inconvenient

We'll tell you to skip a listing you love if the drainage is bad. That's cost us sales — and earned us referrals.
